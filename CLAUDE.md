# Operating rules for this repository

This is Anna Hix's writing repo for the Divergence Series papers
and supporting notes. Prose here is published under her name; the
rules below keep it hers and keep it true. Her merge to main is
the act of adoption.

## Voice

- No em dashes anywhere. Use periods, commas, colons.
- Lead with the finding. No throat-clearing openers.
- Negation-then-assertion is the house move ("This is not X. It
  is Y."). Short declaratives dominate; at most one long
  enumerating sentence per passage.
- First person. She owns claims; never "it can be observed that".
- Minimal hedging. Concrete referents (dates, versions, named
  artifacts) over abstractions.

## Numbers and claims

- Every number must appear verbatim in a frozen artifact of its
  source repository (for the convergence note and drift-monitor
  material: annawhooo/self-context-calibration). Never restate,
  round, or re-derive a number the artifact does not carry.
- Any statement about the status of a PR, merge, or prior
  decision must be verified against the live repo in the same
  turn it is made, never recalled from memory. If it cannot be
  verified right now, label it unverified.

## Change discipline

- Claude sessions never merge PRs here unless the operator asks
  in the session, and never force-push.
- Published or dated documents stand unedited. Corrections are
  new dated notes or clearly dated notices, never silent edits.
